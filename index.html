About

Dr. Maria Zapata

Founder & Principal Consultant, Humanly Works.

I started Humanly Works after years of researching what organizations often overlook: the conditions that allow people to do their best work. This project grew out of doctoral research on disability, gender, and employment — fieldwork carried out in Scotland and Peru studying how skilled professionals navigate work alongside chronic pain, caregiving, and organizational expectations. Now based in Canada, I continue building on it, with a growing focus on neurodiversity and the invisible costs of presenteeism.

One conviction came out of all of it: when people are seen as whole human beings, not managed as variables, organizations win too. Performance goes up. Attrition goes down. And the business case takes care of itself.
